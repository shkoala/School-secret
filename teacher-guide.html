The Midnight Feast

Три связанные сцены · A2+–B1 · 12–13 лет · 36 решений · 9 поисковых улик · 6 собственных ответов

Открыть игру

Как провести

Примерно 50–75 минут для трёх сцен, если грамматика уже изучена. Играйте командой на общем экране либо в парах. В каждой сцене сначала найдите три улики, затем выполните задания и произнесите или запишите два своих ответа.

Правильные введённые слова проверяются автоматически, после подтверждения открывается следующий шаг. Проверку также можно вызвать кнопкой или Enter. Для выбора и сборки предложений нажмите Check & continue, затем Continue. Сюжетные часы не ограничивают время на английский.

После каждой сцены кнопка Enter scene продолжает ту же игру: все улики и ответы остаются в дневнике. Прогресс сохраняется в браузере. Сохранения одной и двух сцен принимаются без потери прогресса. В третьей сцене после собственных ответов нажмите Unlock the archive, затем откройте ящик в картинке или кнопкой Open the archive drawer. Открытые ответы не оцениваются автоматически: проверьте их с учителем или по образцам.

Фокус второй сцены

Present Simple — привычки и состояния; Present Continuous — действие сейчас. Think означает мнение в I think, но процесс в I am thinking about. Have означает обладание в He has a ring, но действие в He is having a meeting. Камера фиксирует вампира; зеркало — нет. Это установленное правило именно этой истории.

Фокус третьей сцены

There is / are: единственное и множественное число, вопрос Is there и отрицание aren’t any. Восемь предлогов места даны на отдельных схемах без ответа в заголовке: in front of, on, in, under, next to, behind, opposite, above. Для opposite использован вид сверху: двери находятся на противоположных стенах.

1. The last normal hour

Find a disguise, read the challenge and check tonight’s timetable. Choose when it is safe to leave your hiding place.

  1. The party takes place ___ 31 October.
    on
    We use on with dates: on 31 October.
  2. The headmaster never eats in the dining hall ___ lunchtime.
    at
    At lunchtime, at midnight, at night.
  3. The school holds a Halloween party ___ October.
    in
    We use in with months: in October.
  4. Build a sentence about the caretaker.
    the caretaker usually checks the doors / usually the caretaker checks the doors
    Usually goes before checks: The caretaker usually checks the doors.
  5. Build a sentence about the headmaster.
    the headmaster is never late
    Is never: frequency adverbs normally follow be.
  6. On ordinary school days, the caretaker ___ the gate at ten.
    locks
    Present Simple describes a routine. He locks.
  7. Which frequency scale goes from most frequent to least frequent?
    always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never
    Always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never. Rarely means almost never.
  8. The party ends at half past ten. Which time is that?
    22:30
    Half past ten is 10:30 p.m., or 22:30.
  9. Write the missing word: 22:45 is a quarter ___ eleven.
    to
    A quarter to eleven = 10:45 p.m.
  10. Write the missing word: 23:15 is a quarter ___ eleven.
    past
    A quarter past eleven = 11:15 p.m.
  11. The teacher checks the costume room at 22:40. The caretaker leaves at 22:42. When can you leave safely?
    22:45
    The camera confirms the caretaker has left. A routine alone would not prove it.
  12. Complete the routine: Ruby ___ checks her messages before leaving school — every single day.
    always
    Always describes something that happens every time.

Свои ответы — примеры

Describe two school routines. Use usually and never.
The caretaker usually checks the doors. Mr Vale is never late.

Say when your class meets. Include a day and a time.
We meet on Fridays at half past three.

2. The man in the mirror

Compare normal routines with what is happening now. Examine the mirror, the portrait and the school’s strange rules.

  1. Mr Vale usually ___ at ten, but tonight he is staying late.
    leaves
    Use Present Simple for the normal routine.
  2. Look at the live camera. He ___ guests now. (greet)
    is greeting
    He is greeting guests now.
  3. You can see the guests at this moment. They ___ old-fashioned coats.
    are wearing
    They are wearing describes what you can see now.
  4. Ruby ___ the face in the portrait.
    recognises
    We normally use recognise in a simple form in this meaning.
  5. Correct the verb phrase: “I am knowing his secret.”
    i know his secret / know / i know
    I know his secret. Know is normally stative.
  6. “I ___ he is the founder.” You are giving an opinion.
    think
    I think introduces an opinion.
  7. “Give me a moment. I ___ about our escape plan.”
    am thinking
    Am thinking describes a process happening now.
  8. Mr Vale ___ a red ring. It belongs to him.
    has
    Has describes possession, a state.
  9. He cannot answer the phone: he ___ a meeting right now.
    is having
    Is having describes an activity taking place now.
  10. We ___ a safer place. This corridor is too open.
    need
    We need a safer place.
  11. The guests are not looking at us. Make the contraction: The guests ___ at us.
    aren't looking / are not looking
    They aren’t looking at us.
  12. What is the strongest evidence that Mr Vale is a vampire?
    The camera shows him, but his reflection is missing.
    The live camera and mirror show the same moment.

Свои ответы — примеры

Contrast one normal routine with what is happening tonight.
Mr Vale usually leaves at ten, but tonight he is greeting guests.

Give your opinion, then describe what you are thinking about.
I think he is a vampire. I am thinking about a safer route.

3. The forgotten library

Find the room model, a class photograph and the school charter. Use all eight place prepositions to open the archive.

  1. There ___ a candle on the desk.
    is
    There is + a singular noun.
  2. There ___ two books under the desk.
    are
    There are + plural nouns.
  3. The candle is ___ the desk.
    on
    On describes contact with a surface.
  4. The key is ___ the glass jar.
    in
    In describes a position inside something.
  5. The books are ___ the desk.
    under
    Under means below something.
  6. The mask is ___ the jar.
    next to / beside
    Next to means beside.
  7. The photograph is ___ the candle.
    behind
    Behind: farther away from the viewer.
  8. The portrait is ___ the desk.
    above
    Above: higher, with no contact.
  9. The chest is ___ the desk.
    in front of
    In front of: closer to the viewer.
  10. The two doors are ___ each other.
    opposite
    Opposite: facing across a space.
  11. Ask about the archive drawer.
    is there a diary in the drawer
    Is there a diary in the drawer?
  12. You check the window ledge: it is empty. Complete: There ___ any keys on the ledge.
    aren't / are not
    There aren’t any keys on the ledge.

Свои ответы — примеры

Describe two places in the room model. Use there is and there are.
There is a portrait above the desk. There are two books under the desk.

Ask a question about the library. Then give a negative answer.
Are there any keys on the ledge? No, there aren’t.
